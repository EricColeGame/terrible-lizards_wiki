## Frequently Asked Questions

### What kind of content is discussed in the terrible lizards discord?
The community covers everything related to prehistoric life and modern dinosaur media. This includes recent paleontological discoveries, evolutionary biology, scientific papers, amateur fossil hunting, digital and traditional paleoart, and dinosaur-themed survival video games.

### Do I need to be a paleontologist to join the server?
No. While professional researchers, academics, and geology students participate, the server is open to anyone passionate about prehistoric creatures. Casual fans, video game players, and hobbyist artists are just as active as credentialed scientists.

### Can members buy or sell real fossils on the server?
No. To adhere to international ethical standards and protect scientifically significant specimens, the community strictly bans buying, selling, or appraising fossils for commercial transactions. Discussions focus exclusively on ethical field collection, photography, and legal documentation.

### How do I participate in community gaming nights?
Once you complete basic verification, visit the role-selection channel and opt in to the gaming notification roles. When a game night is scheduled, hosts will post server connection details and lobby voice channels inside the dedicated gaming category.
