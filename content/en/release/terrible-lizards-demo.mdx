## Essential Survival Mechanics and Environmental Dynamics

Staying alive in the wild requires constant vigilance. The demo introduces an intricate metabolism system where simply drinking water carries significant risk. Watering holes are natural convergence points where apex predators frequently set ambush perimeters.

### Sensory Tracking and Wind Direction

Scent detection operates along a 3D particle simulation. When you activate your scent mode, nearby resources appear as distinct color-coded plumes:

* **Blue Mist:** Fresh water sources and clean tributaries.
* **Red Trails:** Fresh blood and wounded animals.
* **Green Plumes:** Edible vegetation, fruits, and nutrient-dense roots.
* **Gray Highlights:** Carcasses and decomposing remains.

Wind direction directly influences your scent footprint. Approaching target prey from downwind prevents them from detecting your presence early, allowing close-quarters ambushers to bridge the distance before their prey can bolt.

| Environmental Hazard | Direct Effect on Creature | Countermeasure / Survival Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tropical Downpour** | Obscures vision, washes away scent trails | Rely on audio cues; seek shelter in dense tree canopies |
| **Extreme Noon Heat** | Accelerated hydration loss; stamina recovery penalty | Rest in shade; reduce sprinting across open plains |
| **Deep Mire / Mud** | Movement speed reduced by 40%; leaping disabled | Stick to stone outcrops and established game trails |
| **Stagnant Water** | Parasite infection risk; temporary stamina debuff | Drink only from flowing river channels or mountain springs |

## Combat Tactics to Master in the Terrible Lizards Demo

Combat in prehistoric simulators is inherently punishing. Healing wounds takes considerable time, and a broken bone or severe bleed debuff can leave your creature permanently vulnerable. Entering every engagement with a deliberate strategy is paramount when playing the **terrible lizards demo**.

### 1. Stamina Conservation Over Sprinting
New players frequently deplete their entire stamina bar attempting to close gaps across open terrain. Sprinting produces loud footstep audio that alerts surrounding players from hundreds of meters away. Conserve stamina by stalking at a crouch and only triggering full sprint when within striking distance.

### 2. Directional Armor and Strike Angles
Attacking an armored herbivore from the front is often fatal for smaller predators. Frontal frills, thickened skulls, and shoulder spikes deflect incoming damage, reflecting heavy bone-break status onto the aggressor. Circling around an opponent to attack their flank or rear underbelly yields significantly higher critical strike multipliers.

### 3. Bleed Mechanics and Attrition
Lightweight predators rely on stacking bleed status rather than raw impact force. By delivering quick biting passes and backing off, you force your target to burn stamina. Running while bleeding multiplies health drain exponentially, forcing the defender to either make a dangerous stand or collapse from blood loss.

| Combat Maneuver | Optimal Target | Risk Level | Tactical Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ambush Pounce** | Isolated, unaware prey | High (miss penalty) | Inflicts immediate stagger and opens heavy bleed wounds |
| **Tail Swipe** | Flanking light carnivores | Low | Knocks down rear attackers and creates escape windows |
| **Headbutt / Gore** | Frontal chargers | Medium | Cancels forward momentum and inflicts bone fracture |
| **Intimidation Roar** | Smaller competitors | Minimal | Drains target stamina slightly and marks territory |
