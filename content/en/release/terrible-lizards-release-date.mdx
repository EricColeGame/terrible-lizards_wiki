## Core Gameplay Mechanics: Surviving the Prehistoric Frontier

At its core, *Terrible Lizards* is designed to depart from arcade-style monster combat in favor of deep biological simulation. Players do not simply fight; they inhabit an organism that must manage caloric expenditure, hydration, ambient body temperature, injury recovery, and territory marking. Survival begins at the juvenile stage, requiring cautious nesting, stealthy foraging, and maternal defense before maturing into a formidable adult.

The game divides its prehistoric roster into distinct ecological niches, each demanding a completely unique playstyle and behavioral strategy to thrive across diverse map biomes.

| Species Category | Example Genera | Primary Food Source | Core Survival Challenge |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Apex Carnivores** | *Tyrannosaurus*, *Allosaurus* | Large megaherbivores, contested carcasses | High caloric burn, slow juvenile growth, target for rival packs |
| **Ambush Predators** | *Dakotaraptor*, *Ceratosaurus* | Small/medium fauna, juveniles | Managing burst stamina, avoiding larger territorial predators |
| **Heavily Armored Herbivores** | *Ankylosaurus*, *Triceratops* | Roots, dense low-canopy flora | Slow baseline movement, reliant on defensive positioning |
| **High-Browser Megaherbivores** | *Sauropods*, *Brachiosaurids* | High tree canopies, massive foliage banks | Enormous food requirements, vulnerable juvenile life stages |
| **Semi-Aquatic Hunters** | *Spinosaurus*, crocodylomorphs | Freshwater fish, shorebound terrestrial prey | Oxygen and moisture conservation, vulnerability on open land |

Unlike traditional games where leveling up grants arbitrary stat buffs, growth in *Terrible Lizards* alters your creature's physical weight, vocal pitch, turning radius, and dietary requirements. A juvenile hunter might effortlessly evade threats through dense foliage, but maturing into a multiton carnivore shifts your gameplay toward patience, sensory tracking, and energy preservation.
