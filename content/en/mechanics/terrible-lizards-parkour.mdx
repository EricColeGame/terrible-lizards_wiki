One prominent technique is the "Crest Boost," which involves jumping at the exact microsecond your hitbox transitions over a ridge or boulder incline. Another foundational trick is the "Tail Pivot." Heavy carnivores struggle with turn radius at full gallop. ## Common Pitfalls and How to Overcome Them

Many beginner players struggle with **terrible lizards parkour** because they miscalculate momentum decay and overestimate jump ranges. A common error is sprinting until the stamina gauge hits zero right as a leap is initiated. A leap with zero stamina truncates your launch power, turning what should have been a clean chasm crossing into a catastrophic plunge.

Another frequent mistake is ignoring camera direction during technical turns. Because reptilian collision bodies are long and pivot around the hips or shoulders, swinging the camera too aggressively mid-jump can clip your tail or hindquarters on canyon walls, abruptly killing forward velocity. Always align your camera smoothly with your intended flight path before leaving the ground.

*   **Avoid Over-Sprinting:** Maintain at least twenty percent stamina reserve at all times to execute emergency vaults or recovery landings.
*   **Watch Surface Normals:** Jumping against slopes steeper than 45 degrees initiates a slide penalty instead of elevation gain.
*   **Buffer Inputs Wisely:** Avoid button mashing; modern traversal engines register buffered jump inputs up to 150 milliseconds before touchdown.
*   **Check Footwear/Talons:** Ensure character builds or equipped trait upgrades prioritize grip stability if traversing rainy or icy biomes.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Terrible Lizards Parkour

### How do I increase my speed while practicing terrible lizards parkour?
To achieve top velocity, chain your sprint jumps onto downward sloping surfaces. This utilizes gravity assistance to push your speed beyond the default flat-ground cap. Avoid jumping on flat terrain unless clearing an obstacle, as jumping on level ground burns more stamina than continuous sprinting.

### Can all species perform advanced terrible lizards parkour moves?
No, move sets vary heavily by weight class and skeletal anatomy. Small theropods and arboreal species possess the highest agility, wall-kick potential, and recovery tools. Heavyweight herbivores and massive apex carnivores trade dynamic parkour agility for raw momentum, health pools, and structural destruction capabilities.

### How does fall damage work during long leaps?
Fall damage is determined by downward velocity rather than total airtime. Holding the crouch input right before making contact with the ground engages a landing roll or slide, converting vertical drop speed into horizontal forward momentum. This mitigates impact shock and prevents the stumbling stun animation.

### Why does my creature keep sliding down rock faces instead of mantling?
Rock surfaces with an incline exceeding the engine's traction angle limit cannot be mantled directly. To climb steep faces, you must locate micro-ledges, execute Wall Kickoffs at acute angles, or use species with dedicated claw-latch climbing abilities. Ensure your creature's stamina is above half when initiating multi-stage wall climbs.
