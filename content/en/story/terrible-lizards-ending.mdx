## Every Major Route and Branching Path in the Finale

The final chapter features three standard conclusions alongside multiple minor variations depending on companion loyalty and ethical choices. Navigating toward the ideal terrible lizards ending depends on your willingness to balance human survival with respect for prehistoric life. 

### The Coexistence Ending (The Preservationist Path)

If players choose to transmit the complete genome library while disabling the defensive perimeter grid, the narrative awards the Coexistence resolution. In this scenario, the protagonist secures the transport ship with a small clutch of juvenile specimens, ensuring the preservation of the species under an independent scientific coalition. 

While this path offers an optimistic outlook for biodiversity, it exacts a heavy toll. Several security officers fall during the retreat, and the closing cinematic reveals that managing wild predators in an unstable global climate creates ongoing diplomatic tensions.

### The Scorched Earth Ending (The Containment Path)

Choosing to detonate the facility’s thermal core triggers the Scorched Earth finale. Driven by fear of ecological contamination, the team triggers a targeted meltdown that incinerates the nesting grounds alongside the corporate laboratory. 

The protagonist escapes aboard the military gunship, observing the burning caldera from above. The tone of this conclusion is bleak and somber. While human population centers remain safe from invasive megafauna, the loss of groundbreaking scientific discoveries leaves the scientific team disillusioned, questioning if fear triumphed over enlightenment.

### The Corporate Extraction Ending (The Mercenary Path)

Players who align with the corporate benefactors deliver the embryonic samples directly to offshore private interests. This resolution rewards the protagonist with financial security and professional exoneration, but community reports emphasize its unsettling moral tone. 

The closing montage depicts private military enclaves militarizing the cloned fauna, showing that human greed often mirrors the ferocity of the ancient beasts themselves.
